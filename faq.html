Common questions.

Who do we contract with?

NAutomation Labs Private Limited, India. We quote, take your PO, invoice and handle export. One company, start to finish.

What quantities do you take?

From samples to production batches in the thousands, and blanket orders with scheduled releases.

Who actually makes my part?

An audited shop in our network. Your contract, guarantee and documents all come from OrderShed.

How do shipping and duty work?

We quote delivered to your door (DAP) in euros. Import duty is paid by you at the current rate.

Is my drawing safe?

We sign a mutual NDA first, and every shop is barred by agreement from dealing with you directly.
