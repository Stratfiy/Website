The OrderShed platform

One system from quote to dispatch. For every part, in every shop.

Every order runs on the OrderShed platform. It prices your part from the CAD file, books capacity in the shop best suited to it, follows the job on the shop floor and keeps the measured results. You see status, inspection data and on-time delivery for each part, and so do we.
